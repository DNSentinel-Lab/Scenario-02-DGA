## Presentation rule

1. explain the configuration/test;
2. show the relevant screenshot;
3. add a short caption explaining what it proves.

Keep screenshots curated. Do not dump repeated troubleshooting captures into the final scenario story, and never publish credentials, keys, tokens or unrelated sensitive account details.
