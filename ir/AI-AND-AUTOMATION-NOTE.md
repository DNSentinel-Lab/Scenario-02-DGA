# 🧠 AI / ML Use During the IR Phase

No AI/LLM command or webhook action was executed as part of this Incident Responder / Defender workflow.

Detection v1.0, ML anomaly output, and prior AI summaries were treated only as supporting context. The IR decision and RPZ containment authorization were based on independently validated raw resolver telemetry plus explicit human approval.

Do not document AI as having authorized or executed containment for this scenario.
